Mahan Fathi

ماهان فتحی

Research Scientist at NVIDIA

I work on post-training large language models at NVIDIA — reasoning through reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) and reward modeling — supervised by Olivier Delalleau.

Before that I did my master’s in computer science at Mila and Université de Montréal, advised by Pierre-Luc Bacon. Along the way I was a student researcher in Montréal under Ross Goroshin: first at Google Brain, on the MonRL team led by Marc G. Bellemare, then at Google DeepMind, on large language models and state-space models.

I’m proud of switching gears — pun intended. Coming from Mechanical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, I decided not to settle for anything less than following my true passion, robotics; reinforcement learning just happened to be a recently popularized means to that end.

Across academia and industry, I’ve built large-scale research infrastructure for deep learning, in areas such as large language modeling, reinforcement learning and computer vision, and I’ve worked as a software engineer, data scientist and team leader on things like user-centric ML, high-throughput streaming microservices and image datasets at scale.

My research interests are pretty broad, but I find research that provokes questions far more valuable than work focused on providing answers.
